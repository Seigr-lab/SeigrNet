DEVELOPMENT MAP / 26 SEPTEMBER 2026

From electrical evidence to Hyphos

Where development stands after the within-boot reference-order comparison. Open each group for its definitions, evidence and remaining work.

Physical results inform Hyphos definitions; required Hyphos operations determine the next SEIGRASM capabilities. This map describes development status, not the whole Hyphos architecture.

The two first-creation paths remain an experimental comparison: Hyphos-defined creation through SEIGRASM, and initial SEIGRASM creation carrying Hyphos into operation. Existing source and component checks do not qualify either path physically. Six electrical states do not determine the number or meaning of Seigsit layers.

Electrical representation, silicon discovery, firmware and time
Electrical senary
Present boundaryRecognition concerns controlled PMIC/ADC observations. Senary record fields are implemented. Sixteen-state calibration overlapped in the tested comparison.
Evidence neededDemonstrate the intended physical representation and operations. These returns do not establish six-level processor storage or arithmetic, or a general limit on sixteen states.
Agnostic silicon use
Present boundaryAArch64 electrical execution is observed on one apparatus. AArch64, x86-64 and RV64IM/Zifencei callable seeds have software checks.
Evidence neededConnect Hyphos capability discovery to verified implementations and compare the same consequences on further silicon. Current electrical controls use known component addresses.
Firmware replacement
Present boundaryThe existing EEPROM loader and initial hardware state remain dependencies; exclusion of other firmware activity is unproven.
Evidence neededIdentify each dependency, implement its required function and demonstrate an entry/control path independent of it.
Seigr time
Present boundaryOrdered local intervals and senary fractions exist. Counter frequency is reported configuration.
Evidence neededIndependent uncertainty calibration and an explicit ERA/UT1 anchor. Local interval checks do not supply an astronomical date.
Decisions for the next experiments
Does extra spacing help?
Discriminating evidenceBoth boots recognized 288/288 candidate medians at each added wait; timing and journal integrity passed.
Decision informedRetain zero added wait as the reference. Complete counts recurred; extra delay has no demonstrated benefit.
What establishes a new conversion?
Discriminating evidenceIn the balanced boot, all 216 omitted-trigger controls retained the last raw A value; all 180 changed-state trials updated after triggering. Recognition alone did not associate a retained value with the current request. Independent evidence of a distinct physical conversion remains a further requirement.
Decision informedQualify exactly that observation or retain the uncertainty. Repeated values, status changes and ordered timestamps alone do not settle it.
Does Hyphos own the operation?
Discriminating evidenceExecute a bounded existing Hyphos operation through SEIGRASM. Change its source and predict the resulting action; include a rejected-source control and independent returned records.
Decision informedEstablish the source-to-opcode-to-consequence relation required by the seed. Extend SEIGRASM for a demonstrated missing capability.
Is Seigsit created and subsequently used?
Discriminating evidenceRecord creation before later data exists, then attachment and independent consumption through the defined layers. Compare the two initial-creation paths under the same declared requirements.
Decision informedChoose or refine the first-creation mechanism from observed behavior; preserve creation and later use as distinct claims.

The interleaved-reference criteria recurred in two boots, retaining guard 2, zero added wait and every round. The reference-order comparison then found overlap under both schedules. The next proposed investigation follows raw response trajectories at the affected request pairs, controlling preceding requests and reading position while retaining zero added wait. Hyphos integration and Seigsit creation remain subsequent investigations. Measurement qualification and Hyphos integration can advance together with their limits stated. Each new run must identify the capability it advances, the changed variable, the observation that can contradict its claim, and the decision its result will change. Acceptance rules stay fixed during validation; further repeats require a stated recurrence question.
